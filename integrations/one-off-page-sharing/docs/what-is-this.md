# What Is This?

If you are reading this, someone most likely sent you a link to a single page of this site, and you wanted to know what it belongs to.

**This is a private collection.** Describe what the collection is in one or two sentences: what it holds, who keeps it, what it is for.

**It stays private on purpose.** Say why. Then say what you do instead: share individual pages with individual people, one at a time, when a page belongs in a specific conversation. The link that brought the reader here serves exactly one page.

**Say whether they may pass it along.** If a shared page would serve a friend, may they forward their link? Answer plainly, because they will wonder.

And if something here landed, tell the person who sent it to you. That is why they shared it.
